Methodology & formulas — click to expand

Generation and demand

Grid demand in year t: demand(t) = baseGen × (1 + loadGrowth)^t. SMR generation is capped by both installed capacity and demand: smrGen(t) = min(smrCapacityOnline(t) × 8760h × capacityFactor / 1000, demand(t)). Remaining thermal generation fills the rest: thermalGen(t) = demand(t) − smrGen(t).

Rollout pathways

Single unit: full SMR capacity comes online in year 0. Phased multi-unit: one unit is added every 2 years starting in year 0. Distributed smaller units: one unit is added every year starting in year 0 (illustrative simplification — a real distributed-siting study should model each site's local grid segment separately).

Emissions

emissionsAvoided(t) = smrGen(t) × 1,000 × emissionFactor (GWh × 1,000 = MWh; MWh × kg/kWh = tonnes of CO₂; shown in tonnes and kilotonnes).

Fuel cost avoided

costAvoided(t) = smrGen(t) × 1,000 × fuelCost (GWh → MWh, USD/MWh). This is gross fuel-cost displacement only — it does not net out SMR capital or operating cost, which the team should add separately once sourced (see Objective 3 cost data in the parameter table).

Grid share

gridShare(t) = smrCapacityOnline(t) / gridInstalledCapacity × 100. The safe-threshold line is illustrative and must be replaced with an actual grid-code or N-1 contingency criterion once the team sources one (Objective 2 data).

What this tool intentionally does not do

It does not simulate frequency response, transient stability, or transmission-level power flow — those require dedicated grid-simulation software beyond this dashboard's scope. Treat the grid-share output as a first-pass screening indicator, not a stability proof.
